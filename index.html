      Chart

      [R] Retrograde
      Moving backwards against the signs. True of the graha whichever chart is read, so in the table it sits on the name.
      [V] Vargottama
      The division has landed the graha back in the sign it holds in the rashi. True of that division alone, so it sits on the chart row, and never on D1, where every graha would qualify. In D9 it is vargottama proper.
      [S] Shubha kartari
      A benefic in the sign on either side of the graha, the 2nd and the 12th counted from it. Nothing to do with house 1, so it reads the same however the chart is rotated, but everything to do with the division, which rearranges who its neighbours are. The yoga proper hems the lagna rather than a graha, and the Yogas tab reports that one.
      [P] Papa kartari
      The same scissors with the other blades: a malefic either side, the nodes counted among them. Commoner than [S], there being more malefics to go round, and it takes the page's red because it is the one mark here that reports harm.
      [Y] Yogakaraka
      A graha owning both an angle and a trine counted from house 1. Counted from that chart's own house 1, so it sits on the chart row and follows the chart when it is rotated onto another graha.
      [X] Exchange of signs
      The graha and the lord of the sign it stands in occupy each other's signs, which is parivartana yoga. About a pair, and about one division, so in the Vimsopaka Bala grid it sits on the sign that division gives.
      [D] Directional strength
      The graha stands in the house it is strongest in by direction: Jupiter and Mercury in the 1st, the Sun and Mars in the 10th, Saturn in the 7th, the Moon and Venus in the 4th. A house, so it moves with the rotation as [Y] does. Shares the green with [S], both being the graha's surroundings helping it. Shadbala scores the same thing as an arc, out of sixty, in its Dig column.
      [C] Combust
      Burnt by being too near the Sun, within the orb Parashara gives for that graha. A real distance, so like [R] it sits on the name.
      [N] Neecha bhanga raja yoga
      On a dignity, not on a graha or a chart: the debilitation it sits beside is cancelled and the graha stands in an angle or a trine. It was a star, which is a footnote - it says look elsewhere, where every other mark here names its own condition.
